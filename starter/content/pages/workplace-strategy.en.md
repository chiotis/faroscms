## Strategy before production

We start with goals, audience, and competitors to settle structure, priorities, and KPIs early.

## What is included

- Interviews with your team and key clients
- Competitor and search analysis
- A sitemap and content priorities

## What you receive

A short document with the site structure, the message for each page, and a delivery plan any team can follow.

## How long it takes

Usually two to three weeks, depending on the size of the site.

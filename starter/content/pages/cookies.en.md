## What cookies are

Cookies are small files your browser stores when you visit a website.

## Which ones we use

The website uses only what it needs to work:

- a session cookie when an administrator signs in
- a browser storage entry for your choice of light or dark theme and, if you ask, for dismissing an announcement

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

## How you control them

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. The website keeps working, with the default display settings.

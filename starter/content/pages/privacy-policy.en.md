## Who we are

The controller of your data is the company that runs this website. Our contact details are in the footer.

## What data we collect

We collect only what you send us through the forms: your name, email, and message. The website does not use third-party tracking tools.

## Why we use it

We use your details to answer your request and, if you ask, to send you a proposal.

## How long we keep it

Messages are kept as long as needed to finish our conversation and are deleted when you ask.

## Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, or complain to the data protection authority. Write to us at the contact address in the footer.

## Maps and videos

Maps and videos are loaded from third parties only when you press the matching button. Until then nothing is sent to them.

A comedy of manners about love, money and first impressions in the English countryside of the early 19th century.

## About the book

The story follows a family of five daughters and the pressure to marry well. Austen’s irony turns every scene into conversation and every conversation into a revelation.

## Why read it

Because it stays fresh: the characters are sharp, the dialogue quick, and the ending is the one you hoped for, which pleases you all the same.

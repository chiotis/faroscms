A children’s book for grown-ups: wordplay, paradox and a logic that is turned upside down with joy.

## About the book

Alice meets a white rabbit, a hatter and a queen who shouts “Off with her head!”. Each chapter is a small reversal.

## Why read it

Because its imagination starts from language: it reads like one long game with words.

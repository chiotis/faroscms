The book that began science fiction, and a story about what we owe to what we create.

## About the book

Victor Frankenstein makes a being and abandons it. The creature teaches itself to speak and to read, and asks for a place in the world.

## Why read it

Because its questions have not aged: what is the responsibility of a maker, and when does a monster begin?

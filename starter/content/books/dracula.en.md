The novel that gave the vampire the shape we know, written as a collection of letters and diaries.

## About the book

The story passes from one character to another through diaries, letters and newspaper clippings, and the threat closes in page by page.

## Why read it

Because its form is still original, and the suspense builds without the tone of its time getting in the way.

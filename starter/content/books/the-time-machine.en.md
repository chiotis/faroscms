The first great time travel novel, and a social parable dressed as science fiction.

## About the book

The Time Traveller arrives in the year 802,701 and meets the Eloi and the Morlocks. The explanation he gives for their society is a warning.

## Why read it

Because it is short and quick, and its ideas still feed literature and film.

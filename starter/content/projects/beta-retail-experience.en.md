## The challenge

Beta Retail wants customers to find products and the nearest store quickly. The old website was slow on a phone, where most visits happen.

## The approach

We designed for the phone first: large buttons, very few steps, and images that load at the right size. The store finder uses a map that loads only when needed.

## The result

The website loads noticeably faster, and customers reach the information they want in fewer clicks.

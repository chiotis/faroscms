## The challenge

Northbridge designs and builds office spaces, but its old website did not show the work. Projects were buried in PDFs, and prospects could not tell what it could do for them.

## The approach

We started from the projects: each became a page with clear facts (type of space, location, duration) and large images. Visitors can filter the portfolio by sector.

## The result

The team now uploads new projects itself in a few minutes, and sales has one place to send clients.

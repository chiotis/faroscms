A company website is no longer just a brochure. It is a sales, service, and trust channel. A yearly review shows where you are losing visitors.

## Why once a year

Audience needs, search engines, and devices keep changing. A short review every year costs less than a big rebuild after the problems have piled up.

## The checklist

**Speed**

- The home page loads quickly on a phone
- Images are the right size and format

**Content**

- Every page says who we are and what we offer
- Contact details are easy to find everywhere

**Accessibility**

- Everything works with a keyboard
- Contrast and alternative text are correct

**Search and conversion**

- Every page has a clear title and description
- Forms are short and they work

## How to score it

Give each item 0 to 2: 0 is missing, 1 is partly there, 2 is fully there. Anything with 0 or 1 goes on next quarter's task list.
